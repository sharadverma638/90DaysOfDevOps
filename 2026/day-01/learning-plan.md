# My 90 Days of DevOps Learning Plan

## Who I Am
I am a fresher from non-it background transitioning my career in tech.

## My Understanding of DevOps and Cloud
DevOps means developers and operations teams working together to build, test, and release software faster and with fewer errors, using automation.

Cloud means using online servers (like AWS) to run applications, instead of buying physical hardware.

## Why I Am Starting This
DevOps is in high demand in India and offers strong career growth along with competitive pay. It also opens the door to remote work, allowing me to potentially work with global companies. I want to build practical, job-ready skills rather than just theoretical knowledge.

## Where I Want to Reach
I want to grow into a stronger, more advanced position within the DevOps field over time, and these 90 days are the foundation for that growth.

## My 3 Goals
1. Build a solid portfolio that clearly showcases my skills and projects
2. Establish a professional presence on LinkedIn and other platforms
3. Land my first job in a DevOps role

## 3 Skills I Want to Build
1. **Core Infrastructure** – Linux, networking, and shell scripting
2. **Automation and CI/CD** – Git, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions
3. **Cloud and Orchestration** – Kubernetes, Terraform, and AWS

## Time I Will Give
- Weekdays: 3 hours per day
- Weekends: 5-6 hours per day

## How I Will Stay Consistent
I will follow the 90-day challenge without skipping days, complete hands-on practicals, and build projects for each topic covered. I will push my completed work to GitHub regularly and share my progress on LinkedIn to stay accountable and visible.
